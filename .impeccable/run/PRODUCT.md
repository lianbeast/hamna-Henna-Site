# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Brides (and their families) booking mehndi for their own wedding day, plus bridal parties and hosts commissioning coordinated henna for weddings, engagement nights, and sangeet. Setting is a mix: local appointments, destination weddings across the US, and multi-day South Asian diaspora weddings (mehndi / sangeet / shaadi). They are deciding whether to trust this artist with the visible art of their wedding.

## Product Purpose

A promotional surface for Henna by Hamna, a bridal mehndi artist. Success is the visitor believing she is skilled, reliable, and worth booking for their wedding, and acting on it (book an appointment / reach out).

## Positioning

A single named artist whose hand, taste, and care are the product — not a generic henna studio. Bridal mehndi is a bespoke commission tied to the bride's day, and the surface must convey that it is one artist's practiced craft.

## Operating Context

Weddings and wedding-adjacent celebrations; planning happens months out. The visitor is comparing artists, wants to see the artist's work, and wants a clear, low-friction way to inquire. The incumbent poster is a promotional/invitation-style single surface.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Confirmed services:
- Bridal Mehndi — full hand & foot artistry
- Bridal party coordination — coordinated designs for maids, mothers, sisters
- Engagement & Sangeet — occasion designs
- Natural / organic henna — organic, clove-free paste for sensitive brides

The earlier poster also listed "Indo-Arabic & modern motifs" and "live henna bars & events"; these were not confirmed and are dropped unless the user re-adds them.

Explicitly NOT confirmed — must not be claimed: the "natural, skin-safe paste applied days before the wedding so the stain runs deepest" timing/chemistry claim, and any general guarantee of natural/chemical-free paste beyond the confirmed "natural / organic henna" service line. Copy may speak to craft and process in truthful, non-claimed terms only.

## Brand Commitments

- Brand name: **Henna by Hamna**
- Artist's name: Hamna
- Contact: phone **301.555.4321**; Instagram **@henna-designer** (no WhatsApp)
- User-specified direction: warm brown and red tones reflecting traditional, rich cultural aesthetics; tone inviting, sophisticated, creative; highlight the artist's expertise in bridal mehndi design.
- Existing tagline "Grace, drawn in henna." and seal "Tradition, drawn by hand — one bride at a time." are incumbent copy that may be kept or replaced by the redesign.

## Evidence on Hand

No photos, portfolio, testimonials, or press exist in the repo. The redesign must not fabricate bookings, reviews, or client claims. Any service imagery or social proof needs real material from the user, or must be omitted / clearly placeholder.

## Product Principles

1. One named artist, not a studio: the hand and the artist's name carry the brand.
2. Truth over flourish: no timing or chemistry claims that were not confirmed; placeholder contact/socials replaced with the real ones.
3. Bridal, first: mehndi for the wedding day is the hero service; other offerings support it.
4. Warm, traditional, sophisticated: the visual world must feel rooted in the cultural tradition the craft belongs to, per the user's stated direction.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No product-specific requirement established beyond web best practices (readable contrast, keyboard/touch usability, respectful cultural representation).
