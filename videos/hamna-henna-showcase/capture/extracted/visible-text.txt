[p] The Artist
[h2] Artistry Rooted in Tradition
[p] I learned mehndi the way most of us learn anything that matters — sitting close to someone who had been doing it for thirty years, asking what each part meant before asking how to draw it.
[p] Every design begins with a conversation. What you are carrying that day, who matters most in the room, what your mother or grandmother would recognise in it. The patterns are traditional because the tradition earns it, not because it is a trend.
[p] I work slowly and I only take a limited number of weddings each season, so nothing I draw is rushed. That is the whole promise: unhurried work, made for one occasion.
